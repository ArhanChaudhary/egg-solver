  Settings
  
The page starts with every available core and builds the h4 table (464 MiB) on desktops, or h3 (232 MiB) on phones, tablets and devices with 4 GB of memory or less; both take about as long to build. Smaller tables make hard cubes much slower. For faster solves, load a larger table made by the native gen (h6 = 1.8 GiB suits a desktop browser).
