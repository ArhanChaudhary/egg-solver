Final source validation

The comparison tables measure commit 229b99f. The later final source 842a5043abfd adds preflight, shared-memory support, the no-panic integration, the measured stream follow-up and the completed egg-solver rename. Its performance was checked separately against the immutable comparison binaries on h11 (1 GiB pages, 40 lanes; stream warmup/window 10/60 seconds at 1T and 20/30 at 48T). The timing tables below retain their original measured provenance. Search node/lookup counts and solution-length histograms match exactly on the verification datasets.

Final stream throughput regressed by more than 0.5%: 1T (-1.28%), 48T (-1.01%). This measured difference remains disclosed; the competitor tables were not measured on this later build.

The project rename passed full native/scalar/ARM/WASM checks and was separately validated for identical hot instructions, required placement and stack frames. Existing environment names remain fallback aliases and legacy tables remain readable.
