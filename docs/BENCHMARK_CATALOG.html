Correctness and validation

Safety separates replay, optimal-length evidence and UB checks. The detailed assessment identifies the audited source and Miri/sanitizer limits. Final-build validation records the older source A/B check; it is not a fresh timing of every later commit.

Historical and rejected work

Adopted changes and their measurements are in the git history (git log). Deleted experiments lists the 213 unmerged experiment branches and tags that were deleted when the history was rebuilt, including 151 rejected or neutral experiments and 59 archived experiments. Old pending statements and file paths describe their snapshot date. Gains have different baselines and are not additive.

Raw rejected/incomplete attempts remain separated from accepted results. An accepted run can still be a tuning or exploratory measurement rather than a headline result. Use each report's inclusion rules and timing definitions, not a raw record count, to reconstruct the published tables.
