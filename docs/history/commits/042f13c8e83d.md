## Original experiment record

Remove bounds checks from the lane hot loop

std::hint::assert_unchecked for sp < MAXD and m < 18 in advance, and for
sp/k_m/face in note_value, resolve_other, resolve_moved and push_child.
An assert!(target < MAXD) in start_iteration (once per iteration) backs
the depth assumption; move indices come from masks that are subsets of
ALL_MOVES. Removes 16 of the 25 bounds-check sites in the hot loop
(about 5 predicted compare-and-branch pairs per node).

Search is unchanged (depth18: 3607979 nodes/cube, lengths 18:100).
