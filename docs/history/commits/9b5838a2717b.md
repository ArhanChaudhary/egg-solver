## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE c11: sort-chunk (not merged), plus the order-replay diagnostics

NEGATIVE c11: smaller sort chunks and more buckets in staged builds (no faster)

Hypothesis: the staged sort's ST_KEYS stage (~37 us of each T = 17 / T = 18
build at 48T) is bound by its 22 tasks of 2048 jobs, and ST_BUCKETS by 64
tasks over 48 threads. So SORT_CHUNK 2048 -> 1024 (43 tasks) and NBUCKETS
64 -> 96 should shorten the builds.

Measured (CUBE_TRACE=1, solve -c 1 -n 400 random_state at 48T, the mean per
build in ms, on fe926ed plus the touch patch):

| build | total | ext | keys | scatter | buckets | publish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T17 main | 0.1536 | 0.0665 | 0.0359 | 0.0139 | 0.0283 | 0.0089 |
| T17 this | 0.1500 | 0.0646 | 0.0371 | 0.0147 | 0.0254 | 0.0083 |
| T18 main | 0.0906 | 0.0051 | 0.0381 | 0.0127 | 0.0274 | 0.0073 |
| T18 this | 0.0915 | 0.0052 | 0.0396 | 0.0154 | 0.0236 | 0.0077 |

The keys stage did not shrink: it is not bound by the work per task.
`sort_setup` (serial: sampling 1024 keys with prefetch, sorting the sample)
and the stage hand-offs dominate. Buckets gained 3-4 us, and scatter lost
about as much. Not pursued. A cheaper `sort_setup` (fewer samples, or the
splitters of the last iteration) is the lever left there.
