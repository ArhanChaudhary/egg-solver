## Original experiment record

Additive cocsep coordinate (rejected: neutral)

One pext over the (sep, co) bits plus two LUTs (8 KB + 4 KB) with a
nibble-additive 4-of-8 rank, instead of two pexts and three LUTs. Class ids
are unchanged because cocsep numbering does not affect the BFS order.

Microbenchmark throughput improved 6% (3.36 vs 3.58 ns per index), but the
search was neutral, probably because the larger LUTs take more L1. Not
merged.

Benchmark (measured by the m1 micro-optimisation subagent, bench stream
20 30 48 16, median of 3, two sessions):

| session | base  | additive cocsep |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| 1       | 625.9 | 624.0           |
| 2       | 630.4 | 627.2           |

-0.3% / -0.5% (noise level).
