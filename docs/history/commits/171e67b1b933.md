## Original experiment record

p5: bucket-fifo with prefetcht0 instead of NTA (negative, -8.2%)

On top of bucket-fifo (the previous commit, = neg/p5-bucket-fifo): the
table prefetch uses _MM_HINT_T0 instead of _MM_HINT_NTA.

Why: with an irregular revisit gap, a lane may come back before an NTA
line is evicted and miss anyway.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 <lanes>, capped, 1 run,
same run for all builds, solves/s):

| build                   | lanes | solves/s | change       |
|-------------------------|-------|----------|--------------|
| main 35c608c            | 20    | 896.0    |              |
| bucket-fifo (NTA)       | 24    | 865.2    | -3.4% vs main |
| bucket-fifo + T0        | 20    | 822.9    | -8.2% vs main |
| bucket-fifo + T0        | 24    | 840.5    | -2.9% vs NTA at 24 |

1-thread solves/s: not recorded.

Conclusion: worse still. Likely T0 pulls the table lines into L1 and L2,
where they evict lane state; the NTA hint had been a +2.6% win of its own
(opt/prefetch-nta).
