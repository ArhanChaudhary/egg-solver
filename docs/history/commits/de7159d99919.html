NEG share: open list under parking_lot::RwLock instead of SpinRw

Replaces the open list's reader-writer spin lock (SpinRw, from l6-share-a) with parking_lot's RwLock. Its reader fast path is the same CAS on one word, but a contended reader or writer spins only briefly (SpinWait) and then parks in the kernel: the tens-of-µs wakeups that made l6 replace std's RwLock. Rejected: -c 1 latency +24% (random_state) to +51% (17-move scrambles) at 48T; bench share within noise.

Search identical (no change to the search; no bad solutions). The hot functions (Worker::rounds, the share thread closure: frame 0x1f40, bench::stream_thread) are unchanged against 7608a28; find_open, publish, build, build_task, finish_build, start_sort and idle_wait_loop change.
