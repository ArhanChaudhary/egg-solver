c4: next_run without #[cold] (negative, -2.7% on ff2b7a4, neutral on 35c608c)

c4's next_run (set up the next run of child jobs) loses #[cold] but stays #[inline(never)].

Why: next_run runs about 47K times per cube; check what the attribute costs or buys.

Stream throughput, 48 threads (bench stream 20 30 48 20):

basebuildsolves/snodes/schange (nodes/s)
ff2b7a4c4 (#[cold])841.32594M
ff2b7a4not cold (this patch)818.32525M-2.7%
ff2b7a4#[inline(always)]823.62546M-1.9%
35c608cc4 (#[cold]), 2 runs897.6 / 890.92764 / 2743M
35c608cnot cold, 2 runs902.1 / 888.32778 / 2735M~0

1-thread solves/s: not recorded.

Conclusion: on ff2b7a4, without #[cold] LLVM laid the search loop out worse (extra spills, a rematerialized TABS base); on 35c608c the difference is gone. #[cold] stays because it is never worse.

Source: scratchpad c4/neg-next-run-not-cold.patch, made on top of the c4 patch (c4 worktree commit 70c221a, the same tree as 11e1de0 on neg-c4-childorder); applies cleanly.
