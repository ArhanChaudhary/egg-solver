## Original experiment record

p5: phase buckets within two fixed lane groups (negative, -8.4%)

Phase buckets as in neg/p5-bucket-fifo, but within two fixed groups of
lanes: a tighter revisit slack (13..29 visits) at the price of 4 loop
exits per round. Search identical.

Why: bucket-fifo lost mainly through the spread of each lane's revisit
gap; smaller groups bound it.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 <lanes>, capped,
interleaved, 3 runs, solves/s):

| build                  | run 1 | run 2 | run 3 | median |
|------------------------|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| main 35c608c, 20 lanes | 901.8 | 896.9 | 894.3 | 896.9  |
| 2 groups, 20 lanes     | 804.0 | 821.6 | 833.0 | 821.6  |
| 2 groups, 24 lanes     | 835.3 | 826.9 | 833.4 | 833.4  |

Change: -8.4% at 20 lanes, -7.1% at 24 lanes.

1 thread (stream 3 12 1 20, 3 runs, solves/s): main 19.42 / 19.42 /
19.42; 2 groups 18.08 / 18.50 / 18.50 (median -4.7%).
TAGE: 0.766 mispredicts per node (main 0.972).

Conclusion: worse than bucket-fifo at 48 threads. The extra loop exits
and the bookkeeping per round cost more than the tighter slack saves.

Source: scratchpad p5/neg-bucket-2groups.patch, made against 35c608c;
applies cleanly.
