## Original experiment record

Steeper job statistic: sum of 8^(16-h) instead of 2^(12-h)

Instrumentation showed 48.7% of nodes are spent before the final iteration
and the final iteration is cut to about 1/2 of an unordered one by job
ordering. Comparing statistics on the 1000-cube batch (final-iteration
nodes/cube): no ordering 3.54M, node count 1.91M, 2^(12-h) 1.725M,
4^(-h) 1.674M, 8^(-h) 1.661M, 16^(-h) 1.663M, 32^(-h) 1.666M,
8^(-max) 1.700M. Weighting saturates around 8^(-h); stored as f32 to avoid
overflow (Job stays 12 bytes).
