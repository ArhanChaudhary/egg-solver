## Original experiment record

simd: hot tables aligned to a 4 KiB page

x-vc found that HOT_TABLES' offset within a 4 KiB page moved with
unrelated edits (896 vs 768 mod 4096 between two builds whose hot
loops were byte-identical), a suspected ~1% confounder of kernel A/Bs
(cache-set and page placement of the move/index tables). Aligned to
4096 it sits at offset 0 in every build, like the hot loops' fixed
offsets (1c31eca). Cost: at most 4 KiB of padding.

Measured on f8a4919 (HOT_TABLES at 896 mod 4096 -> 0), same code:

1 thread, paired (bench stream 10 60 1 24), solves/s:
| build   | runs                                        | median |
|---------|---------------------------------------------|--------|
| f8a4919 | 24.717 25.183 25.133 24.533 25.217          | 25.133 |
| aligned | 24.667 25.183 25.333 24.717 25.183 25.217   | 25.183 |
+0.2% (neutral; one base run failed to report).
