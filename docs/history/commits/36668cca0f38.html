Merge opt/l6-share-2: share mode with few cubes in progress (-5.6% latency, +4.0% share batch)

Three share.rs commits (stream mode is untouched):

  1. few cubes in progress; data structures: builds staged whenever few cubes are in progress, threads keep (at most 2) unsolved cubes and claim there without the open list; mailbox pieces in fixed slots instead of Mutex<Option>; claimed jobs prefetched; rarely written fields on their own lines. (Checked and kept: the open list, spare, bufs, result and counters are uncontended, so a seqlock or epoch slots would not pay.)
  2. the solver as a parameter of the visit loop: Worker::rounds reloaded the table pointer from self on every visit (no noalias); now it is in a register as in the stream loop.
  3. no early 4-move prefixes: the 43254-job sort per iteration cost more than it saved for one cube at a time.
