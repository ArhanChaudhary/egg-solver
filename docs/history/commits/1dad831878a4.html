NEGATIVE x-vc: p18bc-variants (not merged)

From 66a80018509685e68b9bd73249ba520fcebe1217 Mon Sep 17 00:00:00 2001 From: Ubuntu super@claude-cube.usubflgyqkfexnnpr2q2liw1rb.bx.internal.cloudapp.net Date: Wed, 4 Nov 2026 10:51:27 +0000 Subject: [PATCH] NEUTRAL (x-vc): 18-bit cocsep entries, variants p18b and p18c MIME-Version: 1.0 Content-Type: text/plain; charset=UTF-8 Content-Transfer-Encoding: 8bit

Applies on top of p18 (commit 71eadca on main 55e048e: 18-bit packed cocsep entries, corner depth by class: +1.59% median at 1T layout-averaged on 55e048e), two variants that shorten its index chain, both worse than p18:

  • p18b: rows of 70 tetrad separations padded to 72 entries (162 bytes per corner-orientation row, 354 KB instead of 336 KB), so that an entry's byte offset in its row and its bit shift come from the tetrad mask alone (two small parallel table loads): no shr/and on the chain.
  • p18c: p18b, with the entry read from the byte before it and shifted 3 bits less, so that v & 0x1F8 is the symmetry index times 8, a scaled index of the 64-byte sym_edge rows: no shl. Its index path is 1 µop above main's.

The index paths are as intended, but register allocation in the stream loop gets worse elsewhere (p18b: the table base is rematerialized 4 times in the index path; p18c: 25 lea of the table base in the loop vs 17 in p18).
