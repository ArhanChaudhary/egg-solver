NEGATIVE m8: setup_run prefetches the job and child order of the run 4 entries ahead (neutral)

setup_run fetches the job and the ChildOrder of the next run entry while it starts the current run (c7): the job vectors are too big for L2 and the lanes interleave their streams. One entry ahead is too late when entries are skipped (an entry whose child already ran with an earlier entry's group continues at once): in a 1T stream profile setup_run was ~2.5% of the time, with 21% of its samples on the ChildOrder load and 7.5% on the job's. Now it prefetches the entry RUN_PREFETCH = 4 ahead. (Each entry is still prefetched once; the first 3 entries of a part are not.)

asm: setup_run 833 -> 831 instructions; the hot loops are unchanged (instruction-identical, same addresses). Search identical (the order of runs is unchanged).
