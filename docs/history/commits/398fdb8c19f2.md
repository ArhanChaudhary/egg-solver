## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE m8: Worker::rounds keeps only the lane pointers live (neutral to negative)

The pointer walk of 95cbb11 raised Worker::rounds' stack operands from
102 to 124 (static count). This variant keeps only `next` and `end` live
across the visits: the vector's base (for `offset_from` after `on_end`)
and the active count (for the per-round counters and `maintain`'s test)
are read from `self` again where needed.

asm: rounds 1231 -> 1229 instructions, stack operands 124 -> 119. A perf
cpu-clock profile of the loop (share 1T) puts ~2% of its samples on
instructions with stack operands: the spills are mostly the solver's
loop-invariant fields (packed and table pointers, base, max value, row
multiplier, eo mask) hoisted out of the loop and reloaded, which cost
the same as loads from the solver; not the pointer walk's.

1T share (abs.sh: bench random:1000:2026 1000 1 24 share, paired,
alternating) against 7608a28 at its share pad 16:
| rp2 share pad | pairs |
|---------------|-------|
| 0  | +1.43 +0.12% |
| 16 | -0.51 -0.42% |

| 48 | -0.22 +1.08% |
At the best pad (32) the 6 confirmation pairs are negative (5 of 6; median
-0.58%). Not delivered.
