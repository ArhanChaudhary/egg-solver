lanes: work-sharing batch and latency mode (lanes::share)

Stream mode (run_lanes) gives every lane its own cube: ideal for an endless stream, but a finite batch collapses (bench random:1000:2026 with 48 threads x 20 lanes: 187 solves/s against ~933 for the stream), since 960 lanes share 1000 cubes and the batch waits for its slowest cubes, each searched by one lane; a single cube can use only one lane.

lanes::share::Pool lets the lanes of all threads share the work of the cubes in progress:

  • A cube's search is the same sequence of iterations (target T) of sorted jobs as in stream mode, but the job list lives in a shared context. Threads claim jobs in chunks (one atomic per chunk; smaller chunks as the iteration runs out) and start them in their lanes at job boundaries.
  • Iteration T+1 is built (jobs extended, then sorted by the statistics of iteration T) by the thread that finishes the last job of iteration T, so a solution found in iteration T is optimal and is reported at once; lanes still running jobs of a solved cube drop them (lanes are checked once per round after a cube is solved).
  • A lane whose job ends takes, in order: a piece donated to its thread, another job of its cube, a job its thread already claimed, a fresh cube (at most max_active in progress; 1 solves one cube at a time with all threads), jobs of the oldest cube with unclaimed jobs, or the upper half of the faces left at the shallowest node (with two faces left) of a busy lane of its thread, like cube48opt's split_ida_node.
  • A thread without active lanes asks for work in its mailbox; busy threads check that once per round and donate a split of one of their lanes (a copy of the path to the split node).
  • If threads wait at an iteration barrier, the job extension and the sort of the next iteration are done by all of them: extension by chunks of parents, sort as a staged sample sort that gives the order of sort_jobs.

Each job runs exactly once and its statistic is the sum over its pieces, so all iterations but the last do the work of stream mode (unless split pieces sum a statistic in another order); only the final iteration does more (its jobs run concurrently: ~+1% nodes in a batch).

lanes.rs only gets hooks: a Lane::shared flag, tested where a job ends (once per job), makes advance return Step::JobDone instead of starting the lane's next job; the sort key is named (job_key). The new mode has its own visit loop (Worker::rounds, the dispatch of run_lanes); everything else runs at job boundaries or once per round.
