c4: FIRST_PER_JOB = 2 (negative, -5.40% vs -5.76% nodes)

The first part of a split iteration runs about FIRST_PER_JOB children per job (the ones with the largest statistic); 2 (as in opt/childorder) instead of c4's 3.

Why: a smaller first part means fewer runs.

Nodes/cube (normal side forced before split iterations, face bias; combined = all 4000 cubes):

Earlier free-side config, combined: pj1 -2.80%, pj2 -4.00%, pj3 -4.23%, pj4 -3.91%; pj5 to pj8 about -3.4% (1000 cubes only). 48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured.

Conclusion: 3 is the best value in both configs; 2 leaves out too many good second-best children.
