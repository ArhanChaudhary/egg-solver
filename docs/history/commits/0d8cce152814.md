Subject: [PATCH] DIAGNOSTIC (x-vc): 9 cycles more latency on the index chain

Not for merge. Measures what a cycle of index-chain latency costs, as a
calibration for index-path changes. Three dependent `imul x, x, 1` (asm,
9 cycles) on the packed cocsep entry delay everything after it: ttrep
(and so the symmetry conjugation of the edges), the class and the corner
depth (the corner precheck branch and the value). The control build
(branch x-vc-dnolat) runs the same three imuls on `Solver::row_mul`, off
the chain (volatile asm, result unused). Search unchanged.

1 thread, layout-averaged (abl1t.sh 2 dnolat dlat 24 none 1: 2 of the 4
lld-shuffled layouts x 2 paired runs, bench stream 10 60 1 24, solves/s,
base main 55e048e):

| layout | control (off chain) | +9 cycles on chain | pairs       |
|--------|---------------------|--------------------|-------------|
| none   | 24.883 24.067       | 24.283 24.233      | -2.41 +0.69 |
| 1      | 24.800 24.567       | 24.450 24.150      | -1.41 -1.70 |

Median -1.70% (sorted -2.41 -1.70 -1.41 +0.69), mean -1.21% over 4
pairs: about 0.15-0.2% per cycle of index-chain latency, half of the
value chain's 0.35%/cycle (m7's neg-diag-value-latency).
