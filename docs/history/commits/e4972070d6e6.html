p5: phase buckets within two fixed lane groups (negative, -8.4%)

Phase buckets as in neg/p5-bucket-fifo, but within two fixed groups of lanes: a tighter revisit slack (13..29 visits) at the price of 4 loop exits per round. Search identical.

Why: bucket-fifo lost mainly through the spread of each lane's revisit gap; smaller groups bound it.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 , capped, interleaved, 3 runs, solves/s):

buildrun 1run 2run 3median
main 35c608c, 20 lanes901.8896.9894.3896.9
2 groups, 20 lanes804.0821.6833.0821.6
2 groups, 24 lanes835.3826.9833.4833.4

Change: -8.4% at 20 lanes, -7.1% at 24 lanes.

1 thread (stream 3 12 1 20, 3 runs, solves/s): main 19.42 / 19.42 / 19.42; 2 groups 18.08 / 18.50 / 18.50 (median -4.7%). TAGE: 0.766 mispredicts per node (main 0.972).

Conclusion: worse than bucket-fifo at 48 threads. The extra loop exits and the bookkeeping per round cost more than the tighter slack saves.

Source: scratchpad p5/neg-bucket-2groups.patch, made against 35c608c; applies cleanly. Numbers are the agent's own (not re-measured for this record). Not merged; recorded per the rule that every candidate gets a branch.
