Original experiment record

simd: hot tables aligned to a 4 KiB page

x-vc found that HOT_TABLES' offset within a 4 KiB page moved with unrelated edits (896 vs 768 mod 4096 between two builds whose hot loops were byte-identical), a suspected ~1% confounder of kernel A/Bs (cache-set and page placement of the move/index tables). Aligned to 4096 it sits at offset 0 in every build, like the hot loops' fixed offsets (1c31eca). Cost: at most 4 KiB of padding.
