NEGATIVE x-vc: p18-dense (not merged)

From f0eb5fe616a348a1d4c3f266159921f134a99254 Mon Sep 17 00:00:00 2001 From: Ubuntu super@claude-cube.usubflgyqkfexnnpr2q2liw1rb.bx.internal.cloudapp.net Date: Wed, 4 Nov 2026 10:40:04 +0000 Subject: [PATCH] NEGATIVE (x-vc): dense 18-bit cocsep entries (superseded by x-vc-cocsep-18bit) MIME-Version: 1.0 Content-Type: text/plain; charset=UTF-8 Content-Transfer-Encoding: 8bit

The first form of the 18-bit packed cocsep entries: class << 6 | sym_to_rep at bit 18 * cocsep, corner depth from a per-class table. The index path pays ~4 µops more than the 3-byte entries: mov/shr/and to split the bit offset before the load, shrx with a memory source, and/shl for the symmetry row index, depth load. The delivered form (rows padded to 72, entry read 4 bits up) needs ~1-2.

Search unchanged (count.sh random:1000:2026 and depth18 identical to main).

1 thread (bench stream 10 60 1 24, solves/s, B/A pairs):

  • on 55e048e, layout-averaged (4 lld-shuffled layouts x 2; stream_thread at the same offset as main's in every layout): median +1.59%, mean +1.76% (min -0.13%, max +4.00%);
  • ported to 735dd25 (this patch), layout-averaged: median -1.51%, mean -1.46% (pairs -2.49 -1.52 +0.06 +2.74 -6.31 0.00 -3.27 -0.93; offsets matched in 2 of the 4 layouts);
  • on m7's stack 48d9d27 with pinned pads, vs that stack at pad 16 (2 pairs per pad): pad 0 +0.73 -0.26, pad 16 -0.59 -1.39, pad 32 -1.13 -2.40, pad 48 -1.39 -0.82.
