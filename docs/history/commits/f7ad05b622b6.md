## Original experiment record

lanes: count lookups at issue, merged with the node count

NEGATIVE. Against f4d206a (round-6 stack on 9caf0e2).

Count lookups when issued, packed with the nodes into one u64
(nodes low 32 bits, lookups high 32 bits) that is flushed into Stats at
the end of each job and at a solution, so that the resolve paths drop
their `stats.lookups += 1` read-modify-writes.

Search identical, but:
- 1T (stream 10 60 1 20, 3 interleaved runs, solves/s):
  | build        | runs                   | median |
  |--------------|------------------------|--------|
  | lbs (before) | 21.567 21.450 21.383   | 21.450 |
  | cnt          | 21.233 21.450 21.367   | 21.367 (-0.4%) |
  cachegrind (v3) 148.8 -> 150.2 Ir/node (the register allocation of the
  hot loop got worse);
- incompatible with share mode as merged in 78ce33e: share.rs reads
  `Lane::stats` mid-job (at donations and splits), which would then lag.
