NEG share: open list under spin::RwLock instead of SpinRw

Replaces the open list's reader-writer spin lock (SpinRw) with the spin crate's RwLock (default Spin relax). Its readers take the lock with an unconditional fetch_add and undo it if a writer holds it (two RMWs on the shared line per failed try, from every waiting reader), and a writer needs the count at exactly 0, with no writer preference: under the readers of find_open it waits for a gap. Its relax strategy never yields either (SpinRw yields after its spins: with more threads than cores the pool stalled without it, l6-share-a). Rejected: -c 1 latency +12% to +24% at 48T; bench share within noise.

Search identical (no bad solutions). The hot functions (Worker::rounds, the share thread closure: frame 0x1f40, bench::stream_thread) are unchanged against 7608a28.
