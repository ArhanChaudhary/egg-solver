NEG lanes: sort_jobs and sort_runs by radsort

sort_jobs (LSD radix sort of (key, index) pairs, 3 passes of 11/11/10 bits that skip uniform digits, then a gather) and sort_runs (one counting pass, counts fused into the loop that builds the entries) replaced by radsort::sort_by_key (stable LSD radix sort, 8-bit digits, a fresh scratch buffer per call). Same order (checked in the micro-benchmark). Rejected: 2.2-2.6x slower per sort, -1.5% 1T stream throughput.
