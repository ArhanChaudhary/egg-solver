## Original experiment record

NEG symstart: FxHashMap for the dedup map

SymFilter::dedup (the smallest job of each class of equivalent positions,
for cubes with a symmetry and an antisymmetry) keys a std HashMap
(SipHash-1-3) by (u64, u128) class keys; rustc-hash's FxHashMap hashes
them with a multiply-rotate. The map gets faster, but dedup is not on any
benchmark's path, and the change moves code that is: rejected because a
48T stream slowdown could not be excluded.

Gain where it applies (an ignored timing test, not delivered: dedup of the
superflip's 3240 3-move jobs, std and Fx at once on two 1T slots, 3 pairs
of 5 rounds): 798/838/793 us -> 727/766/728 us (-8.5%; computing the class
keys alone is ~540 us of it). The map alone (micro, 8192 keys): 134 -> 40
us. dedup runs once per job level of at most 8192 jobs, before the search,
and never for random states.

What it changes: dedup is inlined into Lane::extend_jobs and, through the
recursive solver (lanes = 0), into bench's thread closure
(bench::run::{closure#1}::{closure#0}), whose frame goes 0x700 -> 0x6c0:
the stream loop (bench::stream_thread, called from it, unchanged code)
then runs 64 bytes higher on the stack (the r8/s9 stack-frame hazard).
Worker::rounds, the share thread closures (frames 0x1f40) and stream_thread
are unchanged.
