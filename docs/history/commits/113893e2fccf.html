NEG share: SpinRw waits with crossbeam's Backoff

SpinRw's wait (spin_wait: one spin_loop per check, yield after 256 checks) replaced by crossbeam_utils::Backoff::snooze (1, 2, 4 ... 64 spin_loops per check, yield after 7 checks, i.e. 127 spins). Exponential backoff cuts the traffic on a contended line, but the open list's lock is held only briefly (publish, find_open's scan and cleanup): a waiter in a 64-pause snooze sees the release late, and a writer sees the last reader leave late. Rejected: -c 1 latency +3% to +7% at 48T (7 of 8 depth-17 pairs slower); bench share within noise.

Search identical (no bad solutions). The hot functions (Worker::rounds, the share thread closure: frame 0x1f40, bench::stream_thread) are unchanged against 7608a28; publish, find_open, restart and idle_wait_loop change (the lock is inlined there).
