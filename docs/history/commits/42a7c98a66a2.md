## Original experiment record

Edge-only symmetry conjugation for the table index

Replace transform_edges + edge_masks (two full 256-bit composes, the USED
blend and three shift+movemask) in Solver::index with
coord::edge_key_sym(c, t), which builds only the edge classes and
orientations of the conjugate:

  y = vpshufb(Q_t, c) ^ c   Q_t classifies piece j of T (bit 7 E-slice,
                            bit 6 S-slice, bit 4 eo of T); xor adds c's eo
  z = vpshufb(y, P_t)       P_t = Tinv on the edge lane, 0x80 elsewhere
  E = movemask(z), S = movemask(z+z), eo = movemask(sllw(z^P, 3))

Q_t/P_t share one 64-byte line per symmetry (SYM_EDGE, generated in
build.rs). All masks land in bits 16..28 with nothing else set, so
pext(S, E ^ 0x0FFF0000) needs no shifts. Bounds checks on SYM_T and RANK8
are gone. A unit test checks edge_key_sym == edge_key(transform_edges) for
500 cubes x 48 symmetries.

Search is unchanged (depth18: 3607979 nodes/cube, 3053364 lookups/cube,
lengths 18:100).
