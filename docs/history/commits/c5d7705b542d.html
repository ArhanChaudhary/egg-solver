share: no early 4-move prefixes

With few cubes in progress, iterations from 15 on used 4-move job prefixes (43254 jobs) instead of stream mode's 2 and 3. Their tails got shorter, but every such iteration paid a 43254-job sort and per-job overhead (most of the jobs are pruned at iteration 15), and paired measurements (below) show a net loss for one cube at a time. Prefixes follow Solver::job_len again; builds are still staged and threads still keep their cubes when few cubes are in progress (the module doc says so now).
