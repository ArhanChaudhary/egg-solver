## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE m8: index without the multiply after the msplit lookup, both fix-up candidates in apply_move, runs prefetched 4 ahead (neutral as a stack)

Three latency/stall cuts, measured as one stack on top of m8's unsafe pass
+ rp (r9F, def2cd0):
- ix: `coord::index_sym_rows`: msplit comes from a per-k table already
  shifted to its place in the hk index (`MSPLIT_ROWS[k][x] = RANK8[x] <<
  (k + 9)`, a 12 KB static of which 1 KB is used), and the class's row is
  `class * (70 << (k + 9))` in parallel: the index is `row_base +
  msplit_row | eo << 9 | eslot`, with no 3-cycle imul after the msplit
  lookup (cube -> index -> prefetch chain -3 cycles; the calibration of
  0.15-0.2% per cycle predicted ~+0.5% at 1T). Portable twin
  (`edge_parts_sym_rows`), all index tests pass.
- pm (x-vc's parallel mod-3 idea): apply_move adds `move_orients[mv]` and
  `move_orients[mv] - ORIENT_WRAP` (a new 576-byte table) to the shuffled
  cube in parallel: one step less on the X-passed moved-side index chain,
  same µops.
- e1: setup_run prefetches the job and child order of the run entry 4
  ahead instead of 1 (setup_run stalls on them: 21% of its samples on the
  ChildOrder load, 7.5% on the job).

asm: stream_thread 1368 -> 1361 instructions but stack operands 146 ->
153; Worker::rounds 1231 -> 1230, 124 -> 128. Cachegrind (v3): 145.9 ->
145.97 instructions per node.

1T, paired against r9F at its pads (16/16), both pads of the stack set to
P (ab1.sh / abs.sh, alternating):
| pad | stream | share |
|-----|--------|-------|
| 0  | +0.00 -1.14% | -0.08 -1.06% |
| 16 | -2.15 -0.65% | +1.31 -3.43% |
| 32 | -1.16 -1.01% | +0.63 -0.29% |
| 48 | +0.54 +7.07 +0.81 -0.34 +0.32 -0.72% (median +0.4) | -0.56 +2.84 +0.34 -0.14 +0.79 -0.15% (median +0.1) |
At its best pad (48) the stack is within the noise; elsewhere negative.
Not ablated further (machine time). The extra spills in both loops
probably eat the shorter chain.

e1 alone was screened earlier, but that build turned out to be a stale
copy of its base (an A/A run: +0.40 -0.48 +1.74 +0.54%), so e1 alone is
unmeasured.
