b2: unified resolve without the phase dispatch (negative, -3.3%)

One resolve for both lookup kinds (pending kind as data, not control), and advance sets up each child without branching on the move kind: the other side's value, when already known (lo for a half turn, tw for an X' child), is selected without a branch, and one wrapping compare tests "known and above r - 1". The visit no longer matches on the phase.

Why: the TAGE simulation of main put 0.285 mispredicts per node on the phase dispatch and 0.157 on the move kind.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 16, interleaved, 3 runs, solves/s):

buildrun 1run 2run 3median
main fe58740681.0685.6685.6685.6
unified resolve664.1663.0660.5663.0

Change: -3.3% (median). 1-thread solves/s: not recorded.

Conclusion: slower. Removing the dispatch lost in all three b2 attempts (here, the resolve-only variant -3.8%, on skip-sibling -4.5%): Zen 5 predicts it far better than the simulator does, and the unified code adds blends to every resolution. The move-kind part survived in another form (branch-free child setup in skip-sibling, 7a173a1).

Source: scratchpad b2/neg-unified-resolve.patch, made against fe58740; applies cleanly. The same diff is also on opt/neg-unified-resolve (8b59999). Numbers are the agent's own (not re-measured for this record). Not merged; recorded per the rule that every candidate gets a branch.
