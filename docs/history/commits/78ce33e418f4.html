Merge opt/l5-share: work-sharing batch/latency mode and solve CLI

Adds lanes::share. The lanes of all threads share the jobs of the active cubes: chunked atomic job claims, and the next iteration is built by the thread that finishes the last job of the current one, so a solution found in iteration T is optimal. When work runs out, lanes take split-off parts of busy lanes (cube48opt's split_ida_node: the upper half of the faces left at the shallowest node), and idle threads ask busy ones through mailboxes for donated pieces. Threads waiting at an iteration barrier extend and sort the next job list in parallel.

Also adds a share[:C] option to bench (C = 1 solves one cube at a time) and a solve CLI for cube_solver_test-style input: solve <table> -t T -l L -c C [-n N] < scrambles.

Stream mode is untouched apart from a once-per-job shared test.

Checks:

  • check.sh passes (exit 0).
  • Stream search is identical: random:1000:2026 gives 3295521 / 2785769, lengths 15:1 16:35 17:270 18:662 19:32; depth18 gives 3607979 / 3053364, lengths 18:100.
  • Share-mode lengths are identical to main on random:1000, random_state and all random_move sets. Every solution is verified.

Known limits:

  • Share mode on an endless stream runs at about 90% of stream mode.
  • Single-cube latency is about 3.4x the 1.07 ms ideal; iteration builds and the tails of the largest jobs dominate.
  • Job prefixes are fixed at 4 moves.
