a2: job statistic = best root child instead of the sum (negative, +0.3% nodes)

The job statistic that orders the last iteration's jobs (Job::stat, accumulated from the heuristic terms of every lookup in the job) becomes the maximum over the job root's 18 children instead of the sum over the whole job. Each lane keeps one accumulator per root child (cheur[18], slot = stack[1].m) and job_stat() takes the best one.

Why: with the sum (cube48opt's statistic) one promising child is diluted among its ~13 siblings; ranking jobs by their best child should reach the solution earlier in the final iteration.

Conclusion: no gain. The maximum is slightly worse than the sum on both random sets and +3% on depth18, and no p-norm in between is better by more than 1% in simulation. The children of one job still run back to back, so a better job key cannot move a promising child ahead of the rest of its job; a2 found the real gain only by interleaving children across jobs (per-child ordering, later opt/childorder and c4).
