NEGATIVE x-h48: eoesep-fallback (not merged)

From d382cf12f61e6531cbf273ebef75dfca83e79d3e Mon Sep 17 00:00:00 2001 From: Ubuntu super@claude-cube.usubflgyqkfexnnpr2q2liw1rb.bx.internal.cloudapp.net Date: Wed, 4 Nov 2026 08:51:28 +0000 Subject: [PATCH] neg: edge-only (eoesep) bound as a code-0 fallback or folded into the corner bound

nissy-core's H48 looks up a second, edges-only table on every code-0 read (h48/solve.h:133-137: max(line minimum, eoesep)), where eoesep is the distance of the edges to "in their slice and oriented" (782 symmetry classes x 2^11 EO, 4 bits). Count-only experiment (src/exp.rs): an unreduced byte table indexed by the edge part of our key (msplit, eo11, eslot; 73 MB, BFS distribution 0:1 1:6 2:75 3:870 4:9810 5:102520 6:986669 7:8137622 8:34145345 9:27231078 10:349198 11:6), in two modes:

  • CUBE_EXP=1, nissy's rule: code 0 -> max(group minimum, corner depth, eoesep) (h11 only: its index holds every edge bit);
  • CUBE_EXP=3, the edge bound folded into the corner bound at index time (precheck and code-0 fallback, any k).
