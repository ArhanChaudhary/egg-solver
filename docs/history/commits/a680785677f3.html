lanes: drop Node::fmax

The face cut (sibling-face pruning) only depends on the value just learned: when a value v is noted for a child on face f of a node with r remaining moves, every earlier value noted on f was at most r (otherwise f would have been dropped from todo and the child never generated), and nothing is noted on f after it is dropped. So max(fmax[f], v) > r iff v > r, and in the pop path iff the child's lb > r. fmax is never read otherwise: note_value and the pop path lose a load, a max and a store, and the cut no longer waits on the fmax load.
