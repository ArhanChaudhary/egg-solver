share: the round loop visits the lanes by pointer; share pad 16

Worker::rounds indexed its lanes (lanes.get_unchecked_mut(i)): every visit started with imul i, size_of::<SharedLane>() and a reload of the vector's pointer, at the head of the visit's value chain (lane -> pending index -> table word -> value). Now it walks next..end pointers like run_lanes, made again after on_end (which may swap lanes). The visit then starts from the pointer register.

With the previous commit's loop, share mode had lost 1.3% at 1T; with this one it is ahead of main. Pad sweep (both pads set to the value, 1T, 2 paired runs per pad against def2cd0 at its pins 16/0, alternating):
