share: after a solved cube, drop its lanes first, then refill them together

When a cube was solved, each thread restarted its lanes of the cube one by one, and each restart looked for work on its own: another job, fresh cubes, the open list (its read lock) and splits. With one cube at a time that is all 24 lanes of all 48 threads at once, on the lines of the open list's lock, just when the next cube's first build opens its extension tasks (and publishes them under the write lock). The 48-thread trace (CUBE_TRACE=5) showed one thread (sometimes 2-12) running all 18 tasks of the first build's extension, ~50 us, while the later builds' tasks were shared by 44-47 threads.

Now cancel_done ends and deactivates the lanes of solved cubes (and a first pass's useless entries), and then fill_idle refills them, up to the first lane without work.
