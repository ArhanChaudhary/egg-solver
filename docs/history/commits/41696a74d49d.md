## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE: opt-level = 2 for the release profile (neutral at 1T on 78ce33e and cb8cb70)

Toolchain-only change (Cargo.toml). opt-level 2 makes the hot stream closure
much smaller (2994 vs 5168 disassembly lines, 478 vs 1487 stack references,
245 vs 346 conditional jumps: less unrolling and duplication), but it is not
faster.

1T stream: ab1t.sh (paired concurrent runs) and screening group A (the
group's binaries run concurrently, one per L3 slot):
