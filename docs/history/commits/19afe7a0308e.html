NEGATIVE c10: dord (not merged)

NEGATIVE (c10): a one-move extension writes its children in sorted order (dord)

Idea: the T16 -> T17 children inherit their parent's statistic, so their sort order is known before they exist. Sort the 3240 parents (two radix sorts, one per job_weight class), merge the classes' runs, and let the extension tasks write each child to its final position; no keys, scatter or bucket stages (~75 of the T17 build's ~125 us at 48T). The order is identical to sort_serial's (unit test placed_children_match_sort_serial, random stats with zeros and ties).

Result (48T, 2 holds each, paired vs AB = first pass + TAIL_LANES on 735dd25; ms/cube):

Probable cause: order_children is serial work at the barrier (two 3-pass radix sorts of up to 3240 keys and a branchy merge) and the scattered writes of the children; it costs about what the parallel stages did. A parallel merge (or one sort keyed by stat with the rounding ties handled) might still pay; not tried.
