## Original experiment record

a2: exclude the opposite face's half turn on tight bounds (negative, +-0.07% nodes)

When a side's move mask has no half turns (its bound was tight), a move
on face U, R or F of that side also excludes the opposite face's half
turn next (D2 after U, L2 after R, B2 after F): tight_opposite() masks
it out of ALLOWED_AFTER[face] in push_child.

Why: sound extra pruning. The two moves commute, so the word with the
half turn first is an equal word that starts with a half turn, which the
tight bound already rules out. It should remove redundant nodes.

Nodes and lookups per cube (bench <table> <set> N 48 16):

| set              | fe58740 nodes | patch nodes | change | fe58740 lookups | patch lookups | change |
|------------------|---------------|-------------|--------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| random:3000:777  | 3249811       | 3247661     | -0.07% | 2748179         | 2746182       | -0.07% |
| random:1000:2026 | 3295521       | 3297386     | +0.06% | 2785769         | 2787201       | +0.05% |
| depth18 (100)    | 3607979       | 3604920     | -0.08% | 3053364         | 3051409       | -0.06% |

Solution lengths are identical on all three sets (depth18: 18:100).
48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured (pruning change judged on
nodes/cube).

Conclusion: no effect; the differences are noise from a changed
traversal. The case is already covered: exact distances change by at
most 1 per move, so the next node is tight again and its own NO_HALF mask
excludes the half turn there.
