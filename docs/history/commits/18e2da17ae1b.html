NEGATIVE c10: pass-big-first (not merged)

NEGATIVE (c10): a first pass claims its largest last-phase trees first (pass big-first)

Idea: the pass's last phase (T16) sets the pass's critical path (its largest trees: 3-move jobs with a small largest bound), and those entries were claimed only after all lower phases. Knob CUBE_PASS_BIG=b: the first claims are the last phase's first K sorted jobs, K = the jobs whose largest bound + 3 + b <= 16, then the other phases (entry mapping in entry_job, per-entry phase counts in claim).

48T, 2 holds, paired vs the cxl commit (same binary, knob off), solve -c 1 ms/cube unless noted:

testbig3big2
random_state 500+0.4%-2.6%
random_state -c 0-0.1%+0.5%
random_move_15f 200+1.5%+3.2%
random_move_16f 200+5.2%+3.4%
12-14-move scr. 300+1.3%+14.2%
random_state_depth15+2.0%+2.0%

The short sets get worse (their solution is in a low phase, now claimed behind the big T16 entries); random states are within noise.
