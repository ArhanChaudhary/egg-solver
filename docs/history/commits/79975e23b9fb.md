## Original experiment record

Prefetch the moved-side cocsep entry early (rejected: -3%)

In advance(), when the other-side lookup is deferred, also compute the
moved cube's cocsep coordinate and prefetch its packed entry so that
resolve_other finds it in L1.

The extra work is wasted whenever the other side prunes, which is the
common case, and it costs more than the latency it hides. Not merged.

Benchmark (measured by the m1 micro-optimisation subagent, bench stream
20 30 48 16, median of 3):

| build                   | median solves/sec |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| packed cocsep (base)    | 618.9             |
| + early cocsep prefetch | 600.7             |

-2.9% solves/sec.
