## Original experiment record

lanes: speculative prefetch of the next face's first child

NEGATIVE (not for merge). Against cb8cb70 (= fc6d9c9's tree).

Motivation: at 1T the kernel is bound by memory-level parallelism at 20
lanes (16 lanes -9.6%, 28 lanes +10.2%), and most children are pruned by
their first lookup, after which the next child generated is usually the
first move of the next face (an X move, looked up on the other side).
So each issue also computes that child's index and prefetches its line,
a round ahead of its own lookup; with no next face it prefetches the
current line again (no branch). The search is unchanged (prefetches
only).

It is much slower: one extra index computation and prefetch per
generated child (as in neg/m4-diag-extra-index-prefetch) costs far more
than the earlier line saves.

1T (ab1t.sh, paired concurrent runs, stream 10 60 1 20, solves/s):
| build | runs                 | median |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| main  | 21.567 21.750 21.783 | 21.750 |
| spec  | 20.100 20.100 19.950 | 20.100 (-7.6%) |
Stopped after 3 runs; not run at 48T.
