NEGATIVE c11: smaller sort chunks and more buckets in staged builds (no faster)

Hypothesis: the staged sort's ST_KEYS stage (~37 us of each T = 17 / T = 18 build at 48T) is bound by its 22 tasks of 2048 jobs, and ST_BUCKETS by 64 tasks over 48 threads. So SORT_CHUNK 2048 -> 1024 (43 tasks) and NBUCKETS 64 -> 96 should shorten the builds.

Measured (CUBE_TRACE=1, solve -c 1 -n 400 random_state at 48T, the mean per build in ms, on fe926ed plus the touch patch):

The keys stage did not shrink: it is not bound by the work per task. sort_setup (serial: sampling 1024 keys with prefetch, sorting the sample) and the stage hand-offs dominate. Buckets gained 3-4 us, and scatter lost about as much. Not pursued. A cheaper sort_setup (fewer samples, or the splitters of the last iteration) is the lever left there.
