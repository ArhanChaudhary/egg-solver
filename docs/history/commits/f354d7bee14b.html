c4: sticky inverse side for INV jobs (negative, -3.14% vs -3.87% nodes)

Jobs whose statistics came from an inverse-side whole run (INV) are split on the inverse side in the next iteration (partial runs with root.normal = false, todo = mm_i & mask, tw on that side), instead of running whole, ranked by their best child. The sticky part is the INV branch of build_runs' allowed mask and root.normal = !inv in setup_job; the diff also carries the rest of that time's experimental worktree.

Why: let INV jobs use their per-child statistics too.

Conclusion: worse. Splitting on the inverse side costs 18 root children instead of 15, and an INV job's best children are better run together. Superseded by branching every job root on the normal side (-5.74% combined).
