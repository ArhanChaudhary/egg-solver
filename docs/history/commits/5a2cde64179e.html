Original experiment record

NEGATIVE: PGO trained on the exact stream workload, with and without BOLT (-1.3..-1.5% at 1T)

build-pgo.sh: a -Cprofile-generate build, training on the real table with the benchmark workload itself (1-thread stream 3 40 1 20, 20 lanes, and optionally a 2-thread share batch of 100 random cubes, seed 99: the instrumentation counters are shared by all threads, so more threads only add contention), llvm-profdata merge, -Cprofile-use build. The earlier PGO attempt trained 20 s at 16 lanes on an older main; training on the exact 20-lane stream does not change the verdict. PGO shrinks the hot stream closure (2615 vs 5168 disassembly lines; 76 vs 94 cmov, 248 vs 346 jcc) and is consistently ~1.4% slower; BOLT on top of the PGO build does not recover it.

Variants: pgo-s = stream-only profile; pgo-ss = stream + share profile; *-bolt = that PGO build + the BOLT pipeline of the BOLT negative (relinked with --emit-relocs, instrumented, retrained, ext-tsp + hot/cold split).
