lanes: no Idle phase

A lane given a new cube is advanced to its first lookup right away, out of line (refill, using advance_cold), instead of being visited once in an Idle phase. The visit's dispatch then has two arms (one test instead of two) and the round loop no longer carries a third inlined copy of advance.
