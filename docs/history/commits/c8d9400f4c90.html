NEGATIVE p10: h7-base10 (not merged)

NEGATIVE (p10): base 10 instead of 9 for h7 (gen 7 auto). Measured (direct h7 base 9 vs h7 base 10, both validated byte-identical to their references; paired runs, solves/s, stream on one core / one 8-core L3 domain, tables copied to THP memory): nodes/cube random:200:2026: base 9 48,536,433, base 10 46,820,738 (-3.5%) 24 lanes 1T: 2.017 [2.000 2.017 2.000 2.017 2.017] vs 2.233 [2.233 2.250 2.233 2.233 2.233] (+10.7%) 24 lanes 8T: 15.333 [14.933 15.333 15.400] vs 15.267 [15.033 15.500 15.267] (-0.4%) 40 lanes 1T: 2.200 [2.217 2.100 2.200] vs 2.300 [2.300 2.300 2.300] (+4.5%) 40 lanes 8T: 16.867 [14.500 17.100 16.867] vs 15.867 [14.000 15.867 16.267] (-5.9%) With the new default of 40 lanes: better at 1T, worse at 8 threads (the laptop case); a tie, decided by generation time on 8 cores: base 9 ~1 min, base 10 ~5 min (286.5 s of passes + 27 s writing). Not taken.
