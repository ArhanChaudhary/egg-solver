NEGATIVE (c7): keep the solver's free side choice at full-length job roots; inverse-side jobs run whole

Variant of c7-childsplit (on 78ce33e) that does not force the normal side at T=17: a job whose root branched on the inverse side there (normal side tight, ln == r; ~10% of jobs, ~8% of the solutions) keeps JobX::INV; its first split iteration runs it whole on the normal side as one first-part entry keyed by its best child. FIRST 0.1, MERGE 8 steps, no face bias, select-based slot.

Nodes/cube (count.sh): 1000:2026 3089690 (-6.25%), 3000:777 3096967 (-4.70%); combined -5.09% vs -5.68% for the forced normal side with the same FIRST/MERGE (3113996 / 3063220) and -6.98% for c7-childsplit's final setting. Also tried: splitting inverse-side jobs on the inverse side (18 children; the 6 inverse half turns have no statistic): -4.8% (2026, exact slot); giving those half turns the job's best key: -5.2%, the face's best key: -5.4%, best - 1 octave: -4.9% (2026). (Forcing the normal side costs +1.26% nodes in main's search, all of it at T=17.) 1T at this stage (c5, paired perf windows, unpaired with main): +5.0% throughput; superseded.
