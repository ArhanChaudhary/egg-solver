share: count down a lane's ended jobs when it takes a piece

A lane whose job ends refills itself first from a piece donated to its thread. When it took one, the cube of the ended job was never settled: if that job was the last pending one of the thread's claim, the claim was never counted down from the cube's outstanding jobs, so its iteration never ended and all threads waited forever. A piece arrives in a running thread when a donor took its request (WANT -> FILL) just as the thread found work again; with many threads of few lanes this happens within seconds.

Now the ended job's cube is settled (counted down, and left if nothing is pending) after a piece is taken.

New test share_stress: 40 runs of share mode on short scrambles and depth-15 positions with 2..16 threads, 1..5 lanes and 1..unbounded cubes in progress; every solution optimal, every cube reported once. On 78ce33e it hangs (run 9: 16 threads x 1 lane, 2 cubes at a time); with this fix it passes (9 s). The share tests now stop all threads when one panics (instead of hanging).
