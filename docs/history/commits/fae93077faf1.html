Original experiment record

lanes: Lane hot fields first

NEUTRAL (not proposed). Against 0a71855 (m6-6, the round-6 stack on 78ce33e). Data-structure pass, item 1.

Lane is repr(C, align(64)) with the fields of every visit (k_idx, stats, sp, heur, k_m, k_face, k_cd, k_po, phase, target, shared) in its first cache line, the pending cubes k_a/k_b in the second, then the stack, then the per-job and per-cube fields (job_pos, jobs, jobs_tmp, root_c, root_ci, cube_index, started, solution). Before, rustc's order spread the per-visit fields over three lines (0xd40, 0xd80, 0xe00) after the stack. size_of::() stays 3648 (stride 57 lines; no 4K aliasing between the 20 lanes). A test pins the layout. Search identical (verify OK), check.sh OK, share mode lengths unchanged.

No measurable effect: the lanes' hot lines were already L1-resident (20 lanes x ~6 lines per visit is ~8 KB of the 48 KB L1D).
