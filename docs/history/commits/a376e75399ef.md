## Original experiment record

a2: job statistic = best root child instead of the sum (negative, +0.3% nodes)

The job statistic that orders the last iteration's jobs (Job::stat,
accumulated from the heuristic terms of every lookup in the job) becomes
the maximum over the job root's 18 children instead of the sum over the
whole job. Each lane keeps one accumulator per root child (cheur[18],
slot = stack[1].m) and job_stat() takes the best one.

Why: with the sum (cube48opt's statistic) one promising child is diluted
among its ~13 siblings; ranking jobs by their best child should reach the
solution earlier in the final iteration.

Nodes and lookups per cube (bench <table> random:N:SEED N 48 16 and
testdata/depth18; search order changes, solution lengths unchanged):

| set              | b90b706 nodes | patch nodes | change | b90b706 lookups | patch lookups | change |
|------------------|---------------|-------------|--------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| random:1000:2026 | 3295521       | 3306008     | +0.32% | 2785769         | 2794298       | +0.31% |
| random:3000:777  | 3249811       | 3258686     | +0.27% | 2748179         | 2755143       | +0.25% |
| depth18 (100)    | 3607979       | 3715111     | +2.97% | 3053364         | 3143271       | +2.94% |

Simulated job keys (a2's offline ordering simulator, final-iteration
nodes over the same cube set; main's actual value is 1964126):

| job key          | final-iteration nodes |
|------------------|-----------------------|
| sum (p-norm 1)   | 1972115               |
| p-norm 2         | 1945193               |
| p-norm 4         | 1944447               |
| max (this patch) | 1963365               |

48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured (a search-order change,
judged on nodes/cube).

Conclusion: no gain. The maximum is slightly worse than the sum on both
random sets and +3% on depth18, and no p-norm in between is better by
more than 1% in simulation. The children of one job still run back to
back, so a better job key cannot move a promising child ahead of the
rest of its job; a2 found the real gain only by interleaving children
across jobs (per-child ordering, later opt/childorder and c4).
