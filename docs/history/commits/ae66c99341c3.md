## Original experiment record

Batch the other-side lookups of a node (REGRESSION, not merged)

When a node starts expanding (r1 <= base+3), compute the other-side index
of every face with quarter turns to try (X and X' share it) and prefetch
them all in one lane visit; resolve them together on the next visit, then
do moved-side lookups one child at a time. Idea from the search-analysis
subagent (estimated +5-15%).

Measured: fewer lane visits per node and earlier node-level pruning (fewer
nodes are generated), but more lookups, because faces that sibling pruning
or back-jumps would never reach now pay for their other-side lookup.

Benchmark (./bench.sh: stream, 48 threads, 16 lanes, h11 base 10):
