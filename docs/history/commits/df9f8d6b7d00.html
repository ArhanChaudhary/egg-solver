NEGATIVE: PGO of bench, stream mode, re-measured with pinned hot-loop pads (best pad 32: -0.2% at 1T)

a7 measured PGO at -1.4% at 1T (neg/a7-pgo) before the hot loops' placement was pinned (1c31eca), when layout alone moved 1T results by 1-4%. This re-measures it on 7608a28 (x86 code identical to 3a9a50a) with the pads swept: build-pgo-bench.sh trains an instrumented bench on the real table (1T stream 3+40 s with 24 lanes, seed 2026, i.e. the evaluation stream itself, which can only favour PGO; and a 1T share batch random:300:99), merges with rustup's llvm-profdata and builds with -Cprofile-use at CUBEOPT_STREAM_PAD = CUBEOPT_SHARE_PAD = 0/16/32/48.

PGO changes the stream loop (bench::stream_thread: 1387 vs 1368 instructions, 114 vs 92 conditional jumps, 43 vs 49 cmov: profile-driven select-to-branch conversion of data-dependent choices) and is slower at every pad. 1T stream (ab1t.sh, stream 10 60 1 24, paired, alternating start), main 7608a28 at its pads 16/16:
