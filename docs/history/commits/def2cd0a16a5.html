share: the best root-child groups of 10% of the jobs run first in the likely last iterations (top-1 split)

In solve -c 1, 68% of random states end at T = 18. Their solution came 0.75-0.80 ms into T = 18 on average, and mostly from the order in which jobs start: jobs ran whole, by the sum of their per-child statistics. Stream mode orders (job, root child) pairs across jobs (c7). A share-mode port of that (c8, neg/c8-share-child-order) cut nodes by 13%, but its serial run list and per-child records cost more than they saved.

Replay (c11 diag: per-job child statistics and the final iteration's per-child nodes and first solutions of 400 random states, replayed through share's claims at 48 x 24 lanes and 3.2 Gnodes/s; the current order replays to 0.803 ms vs 0.75 measured): T = 18 found time mean / median, runs per cube:

The cheapest form with most of the headroom is one run per job for its best group. The mean stays high because about 60 of 273 cubes find their solution in low-statistic children, after more than 2 ms, whatever the order.

What it does:

  • Leads: at the end of a whole run of a full-length job (normal-side root), lead_of stores the job's lead in Inner::leads (one u32 per job: iteration tag, code of the best child, group). The group is the children with at least half the best statistic, plus the X or X' twin of each, so a separate run never needs X's other-side value. It is computed with a few AVX-512 µops on the child statistics, which lanes.rs now leaves in place at JobDone.
  • Split iteration (the next iteration on the same jobs, built in stages; with h11 T = 18, and T = 19 for 19-move cubes): the jobs stay in place and the claims walk Inner::runs (job index, root children). A first part holds one run per job whose lead code is above the 90th percentile (from the sort's sample), counting-sorted by code in 4-code buckets with ties in job order. Then comes one rest run per job (its other children), in the order of the existing sort by statistic. All of it is inside the existing stages: ST_KEYS reads the leads and counts per chunk and bucket, ST_SCATTER writes the first part, ST_BUCKETS writes the rest. sort_stage_done adds O(chunks x buckets) work. There is no serial pass over the jobs, and every buffer is pooled and touched.
  • Pieces copy the path and its roots' todo, so donated pieces and local splits need nothing more. A job's runs add their statistic atomically.

Soundness: each (job, root child) is in exactly one of the job's runs, since rest = all & !group. A root restricted to some children is a valid node for them (bounds, sibling-face pruning and back-jumps hold for any subset). X' runs only with X. Targets still rise one at a time, so the first solution is optimal. A lead from another cube or iteration (6-bit tag, 1 in 64) only misorders. The unit test split_runs_cover_each_child_once drives the stages and checks coverage and order. lead_of_matches_scalar_lead checks the AVX-512 lead against the per-child definition; the portable lead_of covers the NEON and scalar backends.

Checks: lengths are identical cube for cube on the check sets (short 1000, random_move_15f 200, random_state_depth15 200, random_state 500, depth18 3000; -c 1 and -c 0) and on 120 symmetric positions (symgen) at -c 1 and -c 0.
