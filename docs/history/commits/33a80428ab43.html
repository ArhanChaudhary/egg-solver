lanes, share: no bounds checks, unwraps or division checks on the search, run and job paths (unsafe pass)

User request: use unsafe wherever it removes work, with get_unchecked at the index (not an assert_unchecked hint followed by indexing) and a // SAFETY: line per site; keep anything whose asm gets worse.

Hot loops (bench::stream_thread, share::Worker::rounds):

  • Cube::apply_move_unchecked / premove_inverse_unchecked, and child_lookup as an unsafe fn, index the move tables with get_unchecked (precondition mv < 18); the safe apply_move / premove_inverse assert and call them. Portable twins in src/portable/ (bit-exact; tests cross-check both).
  • push_advance's premove of the pending move had a bounds check per pushed node (pending_move < 18), in both loops: gone.
  • advance / push_advance: the mv < 18 hints are gone (every use of the move is an unchecked index now; both loops' asm is identical with and without them). resolve_other keeps its hints (s8: get_unchecked there cost share mode 1.6%).
  • run_lanes: the per-round lanes[..n_active] slice check and the lane swaps at setup and when a lane runs out of cubes (ptr::swap). Run and job paths (once per run, job or iteration):
  • finish_run, setup_run, setup_job, build_rest (raw writes, no capacity check per entry), sort_runs (an unsafe fn now; no zero fill of the run list), sort_jobs, moves_after, start_iteration's jobs[0]; job_weight masks its index (no check, no unsafe).
  • share: joined/joined_ref (the Local of a joined slot: get_unchecked + unwrap_unchecked) in end_job, take, claim, settle, continue_local, tidy, phase_ended, piece, split, donate, cancel_done; unchecked lane, job, run, statistic, lead and phase-array indexes; division-by-zero hints in entry_job and claim (2 * nthreads); claim's prefetches by address (wrapping_add: a prefetch needs no valid pointer); find_split's stack walk; release_done; ..depth + 1 instead of ..=depth (no overflow check).
  • solver::index / value: SAFETY lines.
