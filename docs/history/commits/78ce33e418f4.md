## Original experiment record

Merge opt/l5-share: work-sharing batch/latency mode and solve CLI

Adds lanes::share. The lanes of all threads share the jobs of the
active cubes: chunked atomic job claims, and the next iteration is built
by the thread that finishes the last job of the current one, so a
solution found in iteration T is optimal. When work runs out, lanes take
split-off parts of busy lanes (cube48opt's split_ida_node: the upper half
of the faces left at the shallowest node), and idle threads ask busy
ones through mailboxes for donated pieces. Threads waiting at an
iteration barrier extend and sort the next job list in parallel.

Also adds a `share[:C]` option to bench (C = 1 solves one cube at a time)
and a `solve` CLI for cube_solver_test-style input:
`solve <table> -t T -l L -c C [-n N] < scrambles`.

Stream mode is untouched apart from a once-per-job `shared` test.

Known limits:
- Share mode on an endless stream runs at about 90% of stream mode.
- Single-cube latency is about 3.4x the 1.07 ms ideal; iteration builds
  and the tails of the largest jobs dominate.
- Job prefixes are fixed at 4 moves.
