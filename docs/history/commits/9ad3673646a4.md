## Original experiment record

lanes: compute the next lane pointer early (neutral/negative)

NEUTRAL/NEGATIVE (not for merge). Diff against 72ade78 (the ring patch,
see neg-ring-roundrobin.patch): compute the next lane pointer at the top
of the visit instead of after it. LLVM spills it and reloads it at the
end, so the next visit still waits on a store-forwarded load.
Not verified with verify.sh (not proposed).
