## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE c9: claims sized to idle lanes (not merged)

NEGATIVE c9: claims sized to the idle lanes in iterations on the same jobs (T>=18)

Idea: with CHUNK_MAX=64 > 24 lanes, a thread's first-wave claim at T18 holds ranks whose chunk position is >= 24.
Those wait a whole job duration, e.g. rank 27 started 0.32 ms into T18. Claim min(chunk, max(idle lanes + 1, CLAIM_MIN)).
48T screening, 500 random states, ms/cube, 3 holds each:
- without tail lanes: p16 1.778/1.815/1.780 vs p16+claims(min 4) 1.860/1.828/1.814 (-c 1, worse); min 1: -c 0 +4.8%.
- with tail lanes (TAIL_LANES=8): scr4 tl8 1.705/1.773/1.717 vs tl8+claims 1.700/1.721/1.744; scr5 1.808/1.737/1.760 vs 1.720/1.703/1.694.
  Nodes/cube fall 3.76M -> 3.66M (-2.6%) consistently, latency is within noise; needs >=7 paired holds before any claim.
