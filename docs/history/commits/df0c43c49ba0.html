NEGATIVE m8: index without the multiply after the msplit lookup, both fix-up candidates in apply_move, runs prefetched 4 ahead (neutral as a stack)

Three latency/stall cuts, measured as one stack on top of m8's unsafe pass

  • rp (r9F, def2cd0):
  • ix: coord::index_sym_rows: msplit comes from a per-k table already shifted to its place in the hk index (MSPLIT_ROWS[k][x] = RANK8[x] << (k + 9), a 12 KB static of which 1 KB is used), and the class's row is class * (70 << (k + 9)) in parallel: the index is row_base + msplit_row | eo << 9 | eslot, with no 3-cycle imul after the msplit lookup (cube -> index -> prefetch chain -3 cycles; the calibration of 0.15-0.2% per cycle predicted ~+0.5% at 1T). Portable twin (edge_parts_sym_rows), all index tests pass.
  • pm (x-vc's parallel mod-3 idea): apply_move adds move_orients[mv] and move_orients[mv] - ORIENT_WRAP (a new 576-byte table) to the shuffled cube in parallel: one step less on the X-passed moved-side index chain, same µops.
  • e1: setup_run prefetches the job and child order of the run entry 4 ahead instead of 1 (setup_run stalls on them: 21% of its samples on the ChildOrder load, 7.5% on the job).

asm: stream_thread 1368 -> 1361 instructions but stack operands 146 -> 153; Worker::rounds 1231 -> 1230, 124 -> 128. Cachegrind (v3): 145.9 -> 145.97 instructions per node.

1T, paired against r9F at its pads (16/16), both pads of the stack set to P (ab1.sh / abs.sh, alternating):
