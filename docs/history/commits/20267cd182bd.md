## Original experiment record

p5: fresh-child fast path on top of regs (negative, neutral -0.0%)

After resolve_moved pushes a child, push_advance generates the child's
first child (always an X move) directly from the node value held in
registers instead of reloading the 128-byte node; the rare kinds go to an
out-of-line advance_cold. Applies on top of p5's regs step (431e46a).

Why: avoid the store-to-load forward of the just-pushed node.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 20, capped, interleaved,
3 runs, solves/s):

| build                          | run 1 | run 2 | run 3 | median |
|--------------------------------|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| main 35c608c                   | 901.8 | 896.9 | 894.3 | 896.9  |
| fresh-child alone (mf)         | 903.0 | 910.3 | 900.8 | 903.0  |
| regs (431e46a)                 | 929.6 | 921.4 | 924.8 | 924.8  |
| regs + fresh-child (this)      | 924.5 | 929.9 | 908.2 | 924.5  |

1 thread:

| build                     | stream    | runs                  | median |
|---------------------------|-----------|-----------------------|--------|
| main 35c608c              | 3 12 1 20 | 19.42 19.42 19.42     | 19.42  |
| fresh-child alone (mf)    | 3 12 1 20 | 19.67 19.67 19.67     | 19.67  |
| regs (431e46a)            | 10 60 1 20| 20.383 20.450 20.533  | 20.450 |
| regs + fresh-child (this) | 10 60 1 20| 20.467 20.400 20.433  | 20.433 |

Change on top of regs: -0.0% at 48 threads, -0.1% at 1 thread. Alone on
35c608c: +0.7% / +1.3%. Instructions per node (cachegrind, x86-64-v3):
alone 170.19 -> 170.10; on regs 168.73 -> 165.08.

Conclusion: neutral on its own. regs already removes the reload (LLVM
forwards the pushed node's fields), so fewer instructions bring no
throughput. Note: the same code was later merged as 236bf33 (step 2 of
opt/p5-sched, 9caf0e2), because together with the no-idle and node-count
steps it measured about +0.9% at 1 thread and +0.6% at 48 threads (noise
level). This branch records the result for the step alone.
