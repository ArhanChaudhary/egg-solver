## Original experiment record

lanes: keep the depth and the top node's bounds in registers in advance

advance() reloaded `sp` and the top node's `todo`, `lb` and `r` from the
stack at the top of every iteration, right after storing them (the resolve's
note_value, the pop's update of the parent): the exhaustion / pop branch
(`lb > r || todo == 0`, ~0.18 mispredicts per node) and the pop's parent
address then also waited for store forwarding. They are now carried in
locals across the loop and written through to the stack (a done node's
`todo` is not written back: it is never read again).
