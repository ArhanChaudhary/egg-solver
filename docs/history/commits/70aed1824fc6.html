m4: diagnostic, 50% random early-resolving branch per visit (negative, -13.2%)

Diagnostic, not a candidate. On top of c6 (the previous commit), each lane visit executes a branch that is taken 50% of the time at random: an xorshift LFSR in a register and an inline-asm test/jz. Its condition comes from registers only, so it resolves early.

Why: calibrate what a mispredict costs in the lane loop (about one mispredict per two visits).

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 20, capped, interleaved, 3 runs, solves/s):

buildrun 1run 2run 3median
c6 (previous commit)877.6879.6884.0879.6
c6 + early random branch768.8761.4763.9763.9
c6 + late random branch742.7746.7747.4746.7

Change: -13.2% (early), about 21.7 cycles per mispredict by the agent's estimate. 1-thread solves/s: not recorded.

Conclusion: a mispredict costs about 22 cycles even when it resolves early. The late variant (neg/m4-diag-brL-incl-c6) costs about 28.
