## Original experiment record

neg: stack and heap placement sweeps of the share and stream threads (tools, no gain)

The experiment hooks used by s9 to find out why r8's `Worker::new` change
made `solve -c 1` 8% slower at 48T, plus the Zen 5 4K-aliasing
micro-benchmarks. Not for merging.

- `hot::with_stack_pad`: runtime stack pads in 16-byte steps from 0 to
  4080 bytes, built from 32 monomorphized frames. Env vars: CUBEOPT_PAD_TOP
  (whole thread: Worker, round counter and every frame below it),
  CUBEOPT_PAD_RUN (everything below `Pool::run`'s frame, not the Worker),
  CUBEOPT_PAD_ROUNDS / CUBEOPT_PAD_IDLE (only `Worker::rounds` /
  `Worker::idle_wait` and their callees), and CUBEOPT_HEAP_PAD (a heap
  block allocated before the lanes). CUBEOPT_DIAG=1 prints the offsets.
- tools/alias4k.c is a pointer-chase load chain with a store in each
  iteration whose address resolves at the same time as the load's. It ran
  at 2.44 TSC ticks per load, and at 6.07 when the store overlaps the
  load's bytes in address bits 11..0 but sits in another page (deltas of
  4096, 8192, 12288 and 65536 bytes; for a 32-byte store, any overlap of
  its 32 bytes). tools/alias4k-early.c shows no penalty (0.61 for every
  delta) when the store address is known early.

Results (48T `solve -c 1 -n 300`, random_move_17f and
random_state_depth17). The metric is the per-cube median of the time ratio
against the reference run in the same exclusive hold. Wall/cube is
dominated by a few randomly slow cubes, which differ from run to run.
- r8's binaries, def2cd0-based: fix3 (`Worker::new`) against main2
  (struct literal), 3 holds: per-cube +3.0% / +2.0%, wall +7.4% / +6.1%;
  fix4 -0.3% / +0.1%. Reproduced. gdb: the Worker is at page offset 0x040
  in both and the lanes heap is at 0xac0 in both. In fix3, `rounds` starts
  at 0xf78 instead of 0xe38 (frames below `Pool::run` +0x140), and the
  round counter is at 0xec0 instead of 0xe68.
- The same +0x140 shift of the frames below `Pool::run` alone (PAD_RUN
  3024 vs 3344 on def2cd0) does not reproduce it: 4 holds give 17f 0.564
  vs 0.565 ms, depth17 0.633 vs 0.631 ms, per-cube +-0.3%.
- 7608a28 PAD_TOP (Worker, round counter and all frames together, 64
  offsets in 64-byte steps; 2 holds for half of them): per-cube -1.1% ..
  +0.9% against pad 0. No consistent winner.
- 7608a28 PAD_ROUNDS in 16-byte steps over 256 bytes, including the
  layout where the round counter aliases a push slot of rounds' callees:
  -1.4% .. +0.6%. Flat.
- 7608a28 Worker at 0x040 / 0xf80 / 0x680 (3 holds): within 1.2%.
- 1T stream, same binary (exp4), stream frame base at page offset 0xfc0 vs
  0x5c0 (PAD_TOP 1344 vs 3904): see the s9 report.
