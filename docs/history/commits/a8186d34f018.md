## Original experiment record

c4: FIRST_PER_JOB = 4 (negative, -3.91% vs -4.23% nodes)

FIRST_PER_JOB = 4 instead of c4's 3 (see neg/c4-per-job-2).

Why: a larger first part catches more promising children early.

Nodes vs main (free-side config of that time; combined = all 4000
cubes):

| FIRST_PER_JOB | 1000:2026 | combined |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| 3 (c4)        | -4.54%    | -4.23%   |
| 4 (this)      | -3.42%    | -3.91%   |

48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured.

Conclusion: worse. Likely the extra first-part children dilute the
ranking: they run before the second part's better candidates of other
jobs.
