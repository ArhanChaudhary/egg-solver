lanes: speculative prefetch of the next face's first child

NEGATIVE (not for merge). Against cb8cb70 (= fc6d9c9's tree).

Motivation: at 1T the kernel is bound by memory-level parallelism at 20 lanes (16 lanes -9.6%, 28 lanes +10.2%), and most children are pruned by their first lookup, after which the next child generated is usually the first move of the next face (an X move, looked up on the other side). So each issue also computes that child's index and prefetches its line, a round ahead of its own lookup; with no next face it prefetches the current line again (no branch). The search is unchanged (prefetches only).

It is much slower: one extra index computation and prefetch per generated child (as in neg/m4-diag-extra-index-prefetch) costs far more than the earlier line saves.
