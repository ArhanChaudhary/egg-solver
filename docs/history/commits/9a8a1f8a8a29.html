NEGATIVE l6: early-4move-prefixes (not merged)

NEGATIVE (l6): share: 4-move job prefixes from iteration 15 on with few cubes in progress

Part of l6-share-2's first commit ("few cubes in progress; data structures") and removed by its last ("no early 4-move prefixes"); this patch re-adds it on top of that series (share.rs only). Idea: with few cubes in progress, use the longest prefixes (4 moves, 43254 jobs) as soon as stream mode's are 2 moves, so that more and smaller jobs share an iteration's tail better. Result (48 threads, 5 paired holds, on 2dbe81d): removing it gave solve -c 1 -n 50 2.338 -> 2.150 ms/cube (-8.0%; runs 2.471 2.305 2.382 2.338 2.297 -> 2.048 2.177 2.150 2.276 2.050), -c 0 +0.3%, bench share -0.0%; 1T -0.1% / +0.6%. Every such iteration paid a 43254-job sort and per-job overhead (most jobs are pruned at iteration 15), which cost more than the shorter tails saved.
