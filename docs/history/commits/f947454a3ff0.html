Original experiment record

share: join a fresh cube before publishing it

Worker::fresh joined its new cube (local, which takes a part of CubeCtx::parts) only after build had published the cube's first iteration. In between, other threads could claim the cube's job, solve it, report it and leave it, dropping parts to 0: local then returned None and its unwrap panicked (an abort in release builds; in tests without catch_unwind the other threads then waited forever for the panicked thread's work, a hang). Easy cubes and loaded or oversubscribed machines make it likely.

Now the creator joins before build: nobody else knows the cube yet, so the join cannot fail.

Tests (unit tests of lanes::share, on a tiny search: corner depths of a cut BFS, Cocsep::truncated, and an all-zero table, H48Table::zeroed; both test-only):

  • share_fresh_cube_solved_by_others_at_once: one-move cubes, one cube in progress, the creator pausing after the publication (a cfg(test) pause of the pool). Fails 5/5 without the fix (the panic in fresh), passes 100/100 with it.
  • share_protocol_with_tiny_search: claims, builds, splits, donations and hand-over with 2..16 threads, 1..3 lanes and 1..all cubes in progress; every cube reported once and as short as Solver::solve. 100/100 on this branch. On cb8cb70 it failed or hung in 44/100 runs (this race, and the piece hang fixed by "count down a lane's ended jobs when it takes a piece"). It also runs under Miri.

Stress on the real h11 table (xsolve, share mode with 1 and 2 cubes in progress, 20000 one-move scrambles per run, 32 threads x 1 lane on 2 cores): opt/l6-share-a aborted in 3 of 20 runs (share.rs "called Option::unwrap() on a None value" in fresh), opt/l6-share in 4 of 20, cb8cb70 in 14 of 15 (16 threads on 4 cores); with the fix (on opt/l6-share) 20 of 20 passed.

No change to the search or to the visit loop (fresh runs once per cube).
