## Original experiment record

NEG gen: Box<[T]> for Generator::derived (changes the BFS relabel pass)

Box<[(H48Meta, &Memory)]> instead of Vec for the derived tables of a BFS
generation (a list fixed at construction; lib.rs builds it with collect,
unchanged).

Rejected (a gen inner-loop change for 8 bytes): the field is read in the
per-depth relabel pass (`derived.is_empty()` and the zip over it in
Generator::run), and its closure (for_word_chunks::<run::{closure#5}>) is
register-allocated differently: 1397 -> 1450 bytes, with a counter spilled
to the stack (gen .text +32, main ca820b6, same folder). bench and solve are
unchanged. Not timed. The logic is the same, so gen output would be too.
