## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE: BOLT on top of PGO (bench): stream -1.3% at 1T, share no better than PGO alone

The brief asked to re-evaluate PGO+BOLT with the pinned-pad methods (a7's
neg/a7-pgo measured -1.3% before the pinning). build-pgo-bolt.sh takes the
PGO bench (profile of neg/pb1-pgo-stream, pad 16), relinks it with
--emit-relocs, instruments it with llvm-bolt 20, trains it (1T stream 3+20
s with seed 2026, the evaluation stream itself, which can only favour it;
1T share random:200:99) and applies ext-tsp block order, hfsort function
order and hot/cold splitting. dyno-stats: taken branches -34.9%. BOLT
moves the hot loops itself (stream_thread: hot part 6184 B at 0 mod 64,
700 B cold), so src/hot.rs's pads do not apply.

1T, paired runs against main 7608a28 (pads 16/16):
| mode | pairs (PGO+BOLT vs main) |
|------|--------------------------|
| stream (ab1t.sh, stream 10 60 1 24) | 24.700 -> 24.367 (-1.35%), 24.617 -> 24.333 (-1.15%) |
| share (abs.sh, random:1000:2026, 1T) | 23.803 -> 24.275 (+1.98%), 23.698 -> 24.113 (+1.75%) |
For comparison, PGO alone at pad 16: share +1.76 +2.80%, at pad 32 +3.19
+3.43%. So BOLT adds nothing to PGO in share mode and costs stream mode
-1.3%. 48T: not run.
