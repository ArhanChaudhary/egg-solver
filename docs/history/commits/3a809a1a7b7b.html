Original experiment record

lanes: compute only the looked-up side's child cube

NEGATIVE. Against 27c4a34 (round-6 stack after nofmax+tabs+cptr, on 9caf0e2).

advance computes only the cube of the side looked up first (X: the other side, X2/X': the moved side) as one composition of k-mask-blended operands (Cube::child_side) instead of both cubes and a blend; the moved side's cube is computed in resolve_other (from the node) and the other side's on a push. Search identical (verify OK).

It saves ~3 instructions per generated child on the native build, but puts the move-kind mask (m -> m % 3 -> sbb -> kmovd) at the head of the child -> cocsep -> index chain, and recomputes cubes on the pass and push paths.
