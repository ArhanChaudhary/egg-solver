Merge opt/s9-pin-v2: solve's share mode at fixed page offsets (4K-aliasing-proof layout)

Zen 5 has 4K aliasing (a load behind an in-flight store with equal address bits 11..0 in another page: 6.07 vs 2.44 TSC ticks), and thread stacks start at a fixed page offset, so frame sizes alone moved solve -c 1 at 48T by up to 8-11% (r8's Worker::new case). solve now runs share mode through Pool::run_pinned: the Worker at page offset 0x040 of a page-aligned slot, rounds entered at 0xe38, and .text.hot_share page-aligned (rounds at page offset 0x310) whatever grows in front of it. bench unchanged.
