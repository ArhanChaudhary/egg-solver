## Original experiment record

share: trace lines buffered, costly counters behind CUBE_TRACE bits

CUBE_TRACE made `solve -c 1` 40% slower (48 threads, 50 cubes: 2.94 and
3.23 ms/cube traced vs 2.16 and 2.10 untraced), so its iteration times
were not those of an untraced run. Almost all of it came from the
per-iteration counters: atomics on the cube's `rarely_written` line,
bumped at every split, donation and settle by all threads. Only ~5% came
from printing the lines while the builds ran.

- The lines go to a buffer that is printed when the pool is dropped.
- `CUBE_TRACE=1` traces times only. The bits 2 (counters), 4 (build task
  log) and 8 (time in `idle_wait`) add the rest (e.g. `CUBE_TRACE=15`).
- New lines: per iteration, when the threads last finished their claimed
  jobs (quantiles); per solution, the solving job's rank, when it started
  in the iteration and its nodes before the solution, the claim pointer,
  and the thread's active lanes.

Trace overhead, solve -c 1 -n 50 at 48 threads (one hold, in this
order, ms/cube):

| CUBE_TRACE | unset | 1     | 3 (+counters) | 5 (+tasks) | 9 (+idle) | unset | 1     | 15    |
|------------|-------|-------|---------------|------------|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| ms/cube    | 2.011 | 2.162 | 2.786         | 2.049      | 2.133     | 2.003 | 2.053 | 2.971 |

A solution's trace line is written by `trace_found` (cold): with it
inlined in `report`, the hot share loop `Worker::rounds` compiled
differently (register allocation; 6307 vs 5976 bytes) and untraced runs
were slower at 48 threads (-c 0 +4.1%, bench share -4.2%, -c 1 +3.7%;
8 paired holds). Found by bisecting the hunks with a build-only check
of `rounds`; with `trace_found` out of line, `rounds` is
instruction-identical to main's.
