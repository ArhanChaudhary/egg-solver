## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE: lanes: cache the job prefix cubes in setup_job (c7)

Consecutive jobs in sorted order mostly share their first len-1 moves
(children of one parent inherit its statistic and stay together), so
setup_job could apply only the last move to cached cubes (stream lanes
only; share lanes change roots between jobs).

Search unchanged (random:20:5 identical nodes). 1T paired A/B
(bench stream 10 60 1 20, solves/s):

| build | run 1  | run 2  | run 3  | median |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| main  | 20.567 | 20.617 | 20.517 | 20.567 |
| jc    | 20.400 | 20.533 | 20.317 | 20.400 |

Median -0.8%: the prefix compare and the 70 bytes of extra lane state
cost more than the three saved moves per side.
