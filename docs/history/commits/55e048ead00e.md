## Original experiment record

share: the measured gain of each optimization in its comment

Each optimization in share.rs now says in a comment what it does and
what it gained. The numbers come from the commit tables of l5 (the share
mode), l6-share-a and l6-share-2, which were paired runs at 48 threads.
Where no paired number exists, the comment cites a single-run screening
ablation and says so, or says "not measured alone". The module doc
summarizes the mode's gains. Comments only.
