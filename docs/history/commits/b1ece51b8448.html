NEGATIVE l6: yield-at-once (not merged)

INCONCLUSIVE (not in the series): share: idle threads yield at once (no pause spinning)

On top of the l6 series (commit "share: the solver as a parameter of the visit loop"); share.rs only.

Screening (78ce33e, S4 + CUBE_SPIN/CUBE_WAIT knobs, one 48T run each): solve -c 1 -n 50 ms/cube by pause spins before yielding: 0: 2.298, 64: 2.424, 1024 (current): 2.538, 16K: 3.194, 256K: 4.213; -c 0: 1.268 1.284 1.303 1.289 1.313. Long pure spinning is clearly worse (spinning cores take the busy cores' boost); yielding at once looked best.

Confirmation on cb8cb70 (48T, interleaved, per-binary holds; a noisy period): r4 c0_48 median 1.1960 [ 1.3070 1.6510 1.1960 1.1810 1.1660 ] r4 c1_48 median 2.4570 [ 2.4570 2.6600 2.3400 2.2420 2.5110 ] r4 bshare median 899.737 [ 815.101 739.042 899.919 907.710 899.737 ] r5 c0_48 median 1.2320 [ 1.3120 1.2600 1.2320 1.1850 1.1850 ] r5 c1_48 median 2.4100 [ 2.4100 2.3320 2.9070 2.2300 2.7850 ] r5 bshare median 899.645 [ 855.699 878.013 899.645 905.123 900.150 ] (plus the S4 runs of the main series: -c 0 1.264 1.400 1.256, -c 1 2.334 2.445 2.404, share 875.8 754.6 856.0) 5-run medians S4 -> yield: -c 1 2.457 -> 2.410, -c 0 1.196 -> 1.232, share 899.7 -> 899.6: no difference beyond this period's noise. Not delivered. (Only the harm of long pure spinning is clear.)
