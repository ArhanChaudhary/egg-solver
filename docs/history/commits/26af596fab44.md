## Original experiment record

Merge opt/c7-childsplit: root children ordered across jobs (-6..-8% nodes, +4.4% 1T)

In the iterations whose jobs are those of the previous one (the likely
last ones), (job, root child) pairs run in the order of a per-child
statistic from the previous iteration, across all jobs, instead of
whole jobs in job order. Details are in the branch commit (21e3ca9).
Share mode keeps whole-job ordering; its job statistic now reads the
per-child sums (2-line merge resolution in share.rs: heur -> cheur).
