share: a first pass claims its jobs in extension order (no sort)

A cube's first build sorted the first pass's 3240 jobs by their bounds (stats_from_bounds, then sort_serial): one thread's serial pass over jobs that the extension tasks of other threads had just written, about 40 us at 48 threads on every cube, while all threads waited for the pass to open. The order matters little: the pass is rarely the last iteration, and its phases overlap without barriers.

Now the pass claims all its jobs as the extension left them; the ones infeasible in a phase are skipped when taken (Worker::take checks the bounds before making the job's cubes). stats_from_bounds remains for first builds that are not a pass.

Screening on top of the previous commit (48 threads, 2 holds; the counting-sort index in neg/c10-first-pass-order, mode 2, vs mode 0): -c 1 -4.5%, 15-move scrambles -9.1%, 16-move -7.4%, 12-14-move -13.3%, depth-15 states -13.4%, -c 0 +0.1%. (Before cxl, the same change was +0.8% on -c 1: the threads' late arrival at the first build hid it.)
