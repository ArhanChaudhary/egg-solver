## Original experiment record

share: one barrier-free first pass for the iterations up to 16

With one cube at a time, the iterations below 16 are cheap (about 9K
nodes in all for a random state at h11) but latency-bound: each one
waits for its build, its tail and its barrier. At 48 threads, T13-T15
took ~0.23 ms and T16 ~0.25 ms of the ~2.0 ms per cube (c9's profile),
and only 17 of 1000 random states end in one of them.

A cube's first build therefore jumps to the first iteration with 3-move
jobs (16 with h11), from any target at most 4 below it, and runs all
these iterations as the phases of one pass:

- Each job is an entry once per phase (phase p has target t0 + p), and
  the entries are claimed in the order of the phases, without barriers
  between them: a phase's tail overlaps the next phases.
- A lane runs its entry as in any iteration: it finds the solutions of
  exactly its phase's length. Such a solution is optimal once the phases
  below it have all ended (every shorter length was searched), and is
  reported then: at once if they had, else when the last of them ends
  (`PassState`). Until then the shortest found is kept, and the entries
  of its length or more are dropped (`take` skips them, running lanes
  drop them at the next `solved` check).
- Phases are counted down per thread, as `outstanding` is (`Local`
  counts per phase; one atomic per phase and thread batch).
- Only the last phase's entries give statistics, for the next
  iteration's sort. New jobs are sorted by their bounds, largest bound
  lowest first. Entries infeasible in their phase are skipped before
  their cubes are made.
- (A job prefix cannot solve the cube: the pass starts only from
  targets above the prefix length.)

The lanes are unchanged (no lanes.rs change; stream mode and verify.sh
are not affected).

c9 had staged variants in which lanes also note solutions shorter than
their target, so that one entry stands for several iterations: one pass
to 16 with targets lowered in place after a solution (p16), one to 15
(p15j3), and a phased one (a 15 entry for all iterations up to 15, then
a 16 entry). None was good everywhere (48T, 3 holds, vs off):

| variant | -c 1 rs | -c 0 rs | depth15 | 15f    | depth16 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| p16     | -11.3%  | -2.4%   | +21.8%  | +22.4% | -44.9%  |
| p15j3   | -3.5%   | -1.2%   | -31.5%  | -31.8% | -29.0%  |
| phased  | -7.4%   | -1.6%   | +13.9%  | +45.5% | -41.2%  |

(A cube a few moves shorter than 15 paid for the whole 15 tree before
its solution could be reported.) With one exact phase per iteration,
short cubes are reported as soon as their own phase finds them.
