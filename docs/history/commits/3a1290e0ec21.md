## Original experiment record

c4: no face bias in the child statistic (negative, -5.48% vs -5.76% nodes)

Drops the +1 quantisation step that c4's child statistic (ck) gives to
children on faces 1, 3 and 5.

Why: check that the bias earns its place.

Nodes/cube (normal side forced, pj3; combined = all 4000 cubes):

| config               | 1000:2026         | 3000:777          | combined |
|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------|
| main                 | 3295521           | 3249811           |          |
| no bias (this patch) | 3141234 (-4.68%)  | 3062944 (-5.75%)  | -5.48%   |
| +1 step (c4)         |                   |                   | -5.76%   |

Older config (1000 cubes): +2 steps -2.88%, +1 step -2.97%.
48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured.

Conclusion: the bias is worth about 0.3% nodes. Likely reason (not
tested): under the canonical move order, a move on face 1, 3 or 5 cannot
be followed by its opposite face, so those children have smaller subtrees
and are cheaper to try first.
