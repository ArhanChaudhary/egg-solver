NEGATIVE x-vc: diag-index-chain-latency (not merged)

From 2ff362cba01b983a75c1e07ae9c33cf8c433795b Mon Sep 17 00:00:00 2001 From: Ubuntu super@claude-cube.usubflgyqkfexnnpr2q2liw1rb.bx.internal.cloudapp.net Date: Wed, 4 Nov 2026 08:36:46 +0000 Subject: [PATCH] DIAGNOSTIC (x-vc): 9 cycles more latency on the index chain

Not for merge. Measures what a cycle of index-chain latency costs, as a calibration for index-path changes. Three dependent imul x, x, 1 (asm, 9 cycles) on the packed cocsep entry delay everything after it: ttrep (and so the symmetry conjugation of the edges), the class and the corner depth (the corner precheck branch and the value). The control build (branch x-vc-dnolat) runs the same three imuls on Solver::row_mul, off the chain (volatile asm, result unused). Search unchanged.

1 thread, layout-averaged (abl1t.sh 2 dnolat dlat 24 none 1: 2 of the 4 lld-shuffled layouts x 2 paired runs, bench stream 10 60 1 24, solves/s, base main 55e048e):

layoutcontrol (off chain)+9 cycles on chainpairs
none24.883 24.06724.283 24.233-2.41 +0.69
124.800 24.56724.450 24.150-1.41 -1.70

Median -1.70% (sorted -2.41 -1.70 -1.41 +0.69), mean -1.21% over 4 pairs: about 0.15-0.2% per cycle of index-chain latency, half of the value chain's 0.35%/cycle (m7's neg-diag-value-latency).
