c4: no face bias in the child statistic (negative, -5.48% vs -5.76% nodes)

Drops the +1 quantisation step that c4's child statistic (ck) gives to children on faces 1, 3 and 5.

Why: check that the bias earns its place.

Nodes/cube (normal side forced, pj3; combined = all 4000 cubes):

config1000:20263000:777combined
main32955213249811
no bias (this patch)3141234 (-4.68%)3062944 (-5.75%)-5.48%
+1 step (c4)-5.76%

Older config (1000 cubes): +2 steps -2.88%, +1 step -2.97%. 48-thread and 1-thread solves/s: not measured.

Conclusion: the bias is worth about 0.3% nodes. Likely reason (not tested): under the canonical move order, a move on face 1, 3 or 5 cannot be followed by its opposite face, so those children have smaller subtrees and are cheaper to try first.

Source: scratchpad c4/neg-no-face-bias.patch, made on top of the c4 patch (c4 worktree commit 70c221a, the same tree as 11e1de0 on neg-c4-childorder); applies cleanly.
