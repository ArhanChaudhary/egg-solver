p5: fresh-child fast path on top of regs (negative, neutral -0.0%)

After resolve_moved pushes a child, push_advance generates the child's first child (always an X move) directly from the node value held in registers instead of reloading the 128-byte node; the rare kinds go to an out-of-line advance_cold. Applies on top of p5's regs step (431e46a).

Why: avoid the store-to-load forward of the just-pushed node.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 20, capped, interleaved, 3 runs, solves/s):

Change on top of regs: -0.0% at 48 threads, -0.1% at 1 thread. Alone on 35c608c: +0.7% / +1.3%. Instructions per node (cachegrind, x86-64-v3): alone 170.19 -> 170.10; on regs 168.73 -> 165.08.

Conclusion: neutral on its own. regs already removes the reload (LLVM forwards the pushed node's fields), so fewer instructions bring no throughput. Note: the same code was later merged as 236bf33 (step 2 of opt/p5-sched, 9caf0e2), because together with the no-idle and node-count steps it measured about +0.9% at 1 thread and +0.6% at 48 threads (noise level). This branch records the result for the step alone.
