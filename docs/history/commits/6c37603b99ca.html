NEG share: idle wait with crossbeam's Backoff

idle_wait_loop's wait for work (1024 spin_loops, then yield, up to 4096 checks of the epoch and the mailbox; tuned by l6: 1K spins 2.54 ms, 16K 3.19 ms, 256K 4.21 ms per -c 1 cube) replaced by crossbeam_utils::Backoff::snooze per check (exponential spins, 127 in all, then a yield per check). Not adopted: no measured gain, within noise in both directions over 8 holds (-c 1 random_state +0.5%, 17-move -1.5%, depth-17 -0.8%, bench share +0.3%, median pairs), and it changes the waiting behavior that l6 tuned (fewer spins before yielding) rather than just replacing code.

Search identical (no bad solutions). The hot functions (Worker::rounds, the share thread closure: frame 0x1f40, bench::stream_thread) are unchanged against 7608a28; only idle_wait_loop changes.
