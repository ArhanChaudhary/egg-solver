## Original experiment record

NEGATIVE: LLVM codegen flags (-C llvm-args=...): none faster at 1T

Toolchain-only change (.cargo/config.toml); this patch carries the
-x86-early-ifcvt variant, all variants are in the table. Every flag below
changes the hot stream closure (lines of its disassembly that differ from
main's, out of 5168, in the "diff" column) but none is faster; the
layout/alignment-only ones move by up to -1%: 64-byte function alignment
alone, which only moves the hot loop (no instruction differs), costs 0.8%,
so about +-1% is the alignment lottery of this loop, not a gain.
Not timed because the hot loop is identical: -enable-post-misched=false
(no post-RA scheduler on znver5), -x86-pad-for-align (10 lines),
-x86-machine-combiner=false (38 lines).
