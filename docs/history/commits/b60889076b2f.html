Original experiment record

lanes: get_unchecked at the index instead of assert_unchecked hints

User request: where an assert_unchecked(i < N) only served to remove the bounds check of the index that follows, index with get_unchecked(i) / get_unchecked_mut(i) there, with a // SAFETY: line saying why i is in range:

  • the lane stack (stack.get_unchecked(depth): the depth stays below the target, which start_iteration asserts is below MAXD) in advance, note_value, skip_xprime, resolve_moved, push_* and child_node; depth + 1 for the pending child's move slot;
  • face_keep[face], allowed_after[face], twin_values[face] (faces below 6), child_stats[root_child] (moves below 18) and stat_pairs[other << 4 | moved] (bounds below 16). resolve_other keeps its hint (depth < MAXD, move < 18, face < 6) and plain indexes, with a comment: the facts also reach the inlined note_value and skip_xprime, and with get_unchecked there the share loop had 39 more instructions and 9 more stack operands (the stream loop 11 and 5), and share mode measured slower (below). The hints that are not for an index stay, with a comment saying so: mv < 18 before apply_move/premove_inverse (their callee indexes the move tables) in job_cubes, advance and push_advance, and the pext range hint of coord::corner_fields.

Verification (built fresh from its own tree):

  • check.sh passes (fmt, clippy with pedantic, tests).
  • verify.sh output identical to main 55e048e: nodes/cube 3058198 lookups/cube 2588380, lengths 15:1 16:35 17:270 18:662 19:32; depth18 nodes/cube 3644301 lookups/cube 3084160, lengths 18:100.
  • cargo asm vs the previous commit (= main 55e048e in the hot loops): no new bounds check or call. bench::stream_thread (the stream loop) goes from 1468 to 1417 instructions, from 5 bounds checks to 2 (the 3 on the lane stack are gone; the 2 left are run_lanes's lanes[index] and lanes.swap at setup and when a lane runs out of cubes) and from 151 to 146 stack operands (same frame). Worker::rounds (the share loop) goes from 1285 to 1229 instructions, from 3 bounds checks to none and from 109 to 105 stack operands; its frame shrinks from 136 to 120 bytes.
