a2: side choice tie-break by the other side's bound (negative, -0.07% nodes)

make_node chooses the side (normal or inverse cube) with fewer allowed moves. This patch compares count * 64 + bound instead, i.e. ties are broken by the other side's lower bound (cube48opt-style). Variants with a real weighting (count * K + bound, K = 1, 2, 4) and the reverse tie-break were measured as well.

Why: on a tie, branching on the side whose other side is tighter could prune earlier.

Conclusion: no effect. Every variant is within 0.1% (noise from a changed traversal); only the reverse tie-break moves the count, by +2.1%. The move count dominates the subtree size and the bound adds nothing.
