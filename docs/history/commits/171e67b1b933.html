p5: bucket-fifo with prefetcht0 instead of NTA (negative, -8.2%)

On top of bucket-fifo (the previous commit, = neg/p5-bucket-fifo): the table prefetch uses _MM_HINT_T0 instead of _MM_HINT_NTA.

Why: with an irregular revisit gap, a lane may come back before an NTA line is evicted and miss anyway.

48 threads (bench /mnt/huge/h11 stream 20 30 48 , capped, 1 run, same run for all builds, solves/s):

buildlanessolves/schange
main 35c608c20896.0
bucket-fifo (NTA)24865.2-3.4% vs main
bucket-fifo + T020822.9-8.2% vs main
bucket-fifo + T024840.5-2.9% vs NTA at 24

1-thread solves/s: not recorded.

Conclusion: worse still. Likely T0 pulls the table lines into L1 and L2, where they evict lane state; the NTA hint had been a +2.6% win of its own (opt/prefetch-nta).
