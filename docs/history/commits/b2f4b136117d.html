tests: hot index path, conjugates in every mode; cross-check tools

New tests (check.sh):

  • solver::tests::hot_index_matches_reference_index: the hot-path table index (Solver::index: cocsep3, packed cocsep entries, index_sym) equals Cocsep::index for k = 0, 7, 8, 11, over random and symmetric cubes, all their 48 conjugates and those of their inverses.
  • conjugates_have_equal_lengths_in_all_modes: the conjugates of a cube and of its inverse have one optimal length, in the recursive solver, stream mode with 1, 3 and 21 lanes, and share mode.

Tools (not run by check.sh):

  • examples/xsolve.rs: solves a cube set (random states, random move sequences, all conjugates of random cubes, or a file) in one mode (stream, share with C cubes in progress, recursive, or ref: an independent plain IDA* using only the cube operations and the table) and prints each cube's length, solution and a scramble; every solution is verified.
  • examples/tabcheck.rs: samples random entries of the h0/h11 table and checks them against their 18 neighbors (no neighbor more than one below: admissibility; codes 1 and 2 have a neighbor one below: exactness), the decode/index round trip and the self-symmetric variants.
  • stress.sh: xsolve in every mode on one set, with per-cube length comparison, timeouts (hangs) and verification.
