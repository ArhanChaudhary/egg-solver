What is retained

The full matrix preserves every original numeric table, source/build screen, page measurement, sample interval, distance-20 check and coverage ledger. It records 230 completed report cells across nissy, nxopt, vcube, supplied cubeopt binaries and cube48opt-rust. The raw index lists the collected run groups; eligible groups and report cells are different counts.

Follow-up corrections and newer evidence

  • Cubeopt audit: the 5.4× / 20.3× arithmetic describes one supplied older Linux executable, not a current Mac release.
  • Large nxopt table: the later 62.474 GB experiment is separate from the smaller nxopt22/31 rows.
  • New nissy throughput: complete-batch wall time on all 500 states with tuned allocations.
  • Safety assessment: validation is bound to its audited source fingerprint, not inferred from timing.

The later 842a504 source had separate h11 A/B validation against 229b99f: stream throughput changed by −1.28% at one worker and −1.01% at 48 workers; the stated latency checks changed by +0.16%, +2.84% and +1.20% time. Those records remain in the full matrix. Historical gains are not added together or silently attributed to the latest checkout.
